To use this module, you need to configure your products:

1. Go to Inventory > Configuration > Routes
2. Activate the "Replenish on Order (MTO)" route
3. Go to your product form
4. In the Inventory tab, add the following routes:
   - Replenish on Order (MTO)
   - Manufacture
5. Create a Bill of Materials (BOM) for the product in Manufacturing > Products > Bills of Materials

When the product is sold in POS and there is insufficient stock, a
manufacturing order will be automatically created and completed.
